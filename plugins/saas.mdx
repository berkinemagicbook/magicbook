The SaaS plugin adds billing to MagicBook. It is the plugin behind the **Finance**, **Affiliates** and **Plans** sections of the app.

## What it adds

- **Plans** with prices, credits and limits. See [Plans](/saas/plans).
- A **free tier** for customers without a plan. See [Free tier](/saas/free-tier).
- **Credits** and prepaid credit packs. See [Credits](/saas/credits).
- **Subscriptions**, **Orders** and **Invoices**. See [Subscriptions](/saas/subscriptions).
- **Payment gateways** for Stripe, PayPal and ten more gateways, plus bank transfer and a wallet. See [Payment gateways](/gateways/overview).
- A **referral program**. See [Affiliates](/saas/affiliates).
- **Finance** reports. See [Finance dashboard](/saas/finance-dashboard).

## Install and enable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    Open **Platform → Plugins**, find the SaaS plugin and press **Install**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable">
    Open **Platform → General Settings → Plugins → SaaS Business** and turn on **Enable SaaS Business**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Follow the setup list on [SaaS overview](/saas/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until you enable it, the billing pages stay hidden from customers and the **Finance** section stays hidden from admins.
