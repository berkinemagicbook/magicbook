Plugins add features to MagicBook without changing the core. They are downloaded from the MagicBook marketplace and installed from **Platform → Plugins**.

## Available plugins

| Plugin | What it adds | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **SaaS** | Plans, free tier, credits, payment gateways, invoices, subscriptions and referrals. | [SaaS plugin](/plugins/saas) |
| **Audiobook Studio** | Full-cast audiobooks from a book or an uploaded manuscript, with M4B and ACX export. | [Audiobook Studio](/plugins/audiobook-studio) |
| **Voice Cloning** | Cloned voices from a recording booth or uploaded audio, usable for narration and audiobooks. | [Voice Cloning](/plugins/voice-cloning) |

Cloud storage plugins for Amazon S3, Wasabi, Cloudflare R2 and Google Cloud Storage also appear on the plugins list. See [Storage](/advanced/storage).

## Install a plugin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plugins list">
    Open **Platform → Plugins**. Use the tabs to find the plugin, or search for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Press **Install** on a free plugin. A paid plugin shows **Buy Now**, which opens the checkout. Once bought, press **Install**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable">
    Open **Platform → General Settings → Plugins** and open the plugin's card. Turn on the plugin's switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it to plans">
    Decide which plans include the plugin on the plan form under **What's included**. See [Plans](/saas/plans).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Plugins that need the license check use the license saved on **Platform → Activation**. See [License activation](/license/activation).

## Update or remove a plugin

- **Update** installs a newer version when **Update Available** shows.
- **Uninstall** removes the plugin's files. Its data stays in the database, so a reinstall brings it back.

## Where plugins appear

When a plugin is installed and enabled, its sidebar item appears for the customers who are allowed to use it. Customers without access see a locked item with **upgrade to unlock**. Audiobook Studio appears under **Books**, and Voice Cloning appears under **Books** too.

## Themes

Themes are a separate kind of extension. See [Plugins and themes](/admin/plugins-and-themes).
