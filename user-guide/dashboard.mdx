**Home** is the first page after you sign in. It shows the book to carry on with, what is left to do in it, and the account at a glance.

## New accounts

If you have no books yet, Home shows **Your first book is one tap away**, with Inkling the owl and three short steps. Each step has a **Done** tick, and the panel can be hidden with **Hide** once you have finished it.

## On your desk

The main card shows the book you were last working on, its progress and its next step. Buttons open the book in its workspace, or start the next step in the book.

Under it, **Also on the go** lists your other books that are still in progress.

## Your rhythm

The **Your rhythm** grid shows the days you worked on your books over the last few weeks. Darker days mean more work. The legend runs from **Less** to **More**.

## Your books in figures

Five counters sum up your shelf: **Finished**, **Books**, **Words** (across all your books), **Pictures** (covers and page pictures) and **Narration** (how long your books have been read aloud).

## Your account

The **Your account** card shows:

- **Credits**: the balance you can spend, or **Unlimited** when your plan includes unlimited credits. A plan allowance shows as **included in your plan**. See [Credits](/account/credits).
- **Credits spent per day over the last 30 days**, a chart of your AI use.
- **Manage plan** and **Get credits** buttons when billing is turned on.

<Note>
The AI cannot write, draw or read aloud for you until you have credits. When the balance is empty, the card tells you so.
</Note>

## AI activity

The **AI activity** card lists the AI steps that are still waiting or working. It refreshes every few seconds while work is in progress. Below it are the latest finished steps and what each one cost. If a step failed, the card shows the reason. See [AI tools](/user-guide/ai-tools).

## Children

The **Children** card lists the children you have added. **Add a child** opens the builder, and **Manage** opens the children page. See [Children](/user-guide/children).

## Media library and notifications

The **Media library** card shows how many files you have uploaded. **Notifications** shows unread messages. The sidebar item **Notifications** opens the full list.
