The reader shows a book as a reader sees it: full screen, page by page. Open it from a book's **Read** button on the bookshelf or from Storyland.

## Read a book

- Turn pages with the on-screen arrows, or the arrow keys.
- **Back to the book** returns to the workspace. In Storyland, **Back to Storyland** returns to the home page.
- **Download as PDF** downloads the book. See [Sharing and PDF](/user-guide/sharing-and-pdf).
- **Share** creates a private link. See [Sharing and PDF](/user-guide/sharing-and-pdf).

## Narration

**Narrate** reads the book aloud. Choose a narrator first. The narrator list shows the voices the admin has switched on, plus your own cloned voice if your plan has Voice Cloning.

- **Hear a sample** plays a short sample of the narrator.
- **Record narration** records the book, chapter by chapter. **Record the rest** records the chapters that are not yet narrated.
- While a chapter plays, **On air** shows it is playing. **Stop after this chapter** stops at the end of the current chapter.
- When chapters have been narrated, you can listen along while the text is read.

Narration needs an ElevenLabs key and at least one voice switched on by the admin. Without them, the reader shows **Narration needs an ElevenLabs key and at least one voice switched on.** The **Set up voices** button is for admins. See [Narration](/user-guide/narration).

If your plan does not include narration, the reader shows **Narration is not part of your plan.** and a **See plans** button.

<Note>
A book with no text yet has nothing to read. The reader shows **There is no text to read aloud yet.**
</Note>

## Pages and chapters

Picture books are read page by page with their pictures. Chapter books and novels are read chapter by chapter. The reader keeps the position you left at on each book.
