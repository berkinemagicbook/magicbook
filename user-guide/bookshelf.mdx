The **Bookshelf** holds every book you have started. Open it from **Books → Bookshelf** in the sidebar.

## Start a new book

Three doors at the top start a book, one for each mode:

- **Storyland** makes a picture book for children, without typing. See [Storyland](/user-guide/storyland).
- **Family** makes a picture book, short story or chapter book for one of your children. See [Family](/user-guide/family).
- **Writer's Room** makes a novel, chapter book, short story or picture book. See [Writer's Room](/user-guide/writers-room/overview).

A door your plan does not include shows **Not included in your plan** and an **Upgrade to open** button. See [Billing](/account/billing).

## Find and sort books

The shelf shows three counters: **Books**, **Words** and **Finished**. Above the books:

- **Show books from** filters the shelf by mode, or shows **All**.
- **Find a book** searches the titles.
- **Sort books** orders them by **Recently opened**, **Title A–Z**, **Furthest along** or **Newest first**.
- **View** switches between **Covers** and **Shelf**. The shelf view puts each book on a spine, one shelf per mode.

Filtering and sorting happen in your browser, so the list changes at once. The line **Showing N of M books** tells you when a filter is active. **Clear** removes the filters.

## Open a book

Select a book's cover or spine to open its workspace. The menu on each book offers:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Read** | Opens the reader, page by page. See [Reader](/user-guide/reader). |
| **Open** | Opens the workspace where the book is written and drawn. |
| **Download PDF** | Downloads a PDF of the book. See [Sharing and PDF](/user-guide/sharing-and-pdf). |
| **Make an audiobook** | Opens Audiobook Studio, when the plugin is installed. See [Audiobook Studio](/plugins/audiobook-studio). |
| **Duplicate** | Makes a copy of the book. |
| **Remove** | Removes the book from your shelf. |

## Storyland and kid mode

The shelf has a **Storyland home** button and a **Kid mode** button. Storyland is the children's start page, and kid mode locks the app into it. See [Kid mode](/user-guide/kid-mode).

## When the shelf is empty

The shelf shows Inkling and the text **Your shelf is empty**. Pick one of the three doors to start your first book.
