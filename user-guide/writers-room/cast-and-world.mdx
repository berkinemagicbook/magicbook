Cast & World is the Story Journal laid out as a wall of cards. It has three shelves: **Cast** for people, **Places** for locations, and **World** for everything else.

## Entries and kinds

Every entry has a kind. The kind decides which shelf it sits on and which fields its dossier has.

| Kind | Shelf | Dossier fields |
|---|---|---|
| Character | Cast | Wants, Needs, Fears, Secret, Looks, Voice, Change |
| Place | Places | Sights, Sounds and smells, Feeling, History |
| Item, World rule, Group, Note | World | Details, Why it matters |

Premise, Plot thread and Style entries do not sit on a shelf. The stages that use them show them in place.

Select a card to open its dossier. Every field saves as you leave it. Each entry can have **Also called…** aliases, a **Role in the story** and a one-line summary, **In one line**.

A fact can be given a **True from** point, so the AI knows whether it was true from the start or only from a later chapter. Add facts with **Add a fact** and remove them with **Remove fact**.

**Hidden from AI** keeps an entry out of anything the AI writes. Use it for a secret the author wants to reveal later.

## Character sheets

A character sheet is a drawn reference picture of a character. Pick an art style, then press **Draw character sheet**. Press **Draw again** for a new one. Picture books use these sheets so the character looks the same on every page. See [Image models](/admin/image-models).

## Fill in the blanks

**Fill in the blanks** asks the AI to complete the empty fields of an entry.

## Auditions

Use **Hold auditions** to find a character. Choose **What kind of character** you need, then press **Suggest three**. The three audition cards show a name and the character's **Wants**, **Fears** and **Secret**. Press **Cast them** to add the chosen character to the Cast. **Clear the auditions** removes the cards.

## Interview a character

Open a character and press **Interview**. Ask questions and the character answers in their own voice. The answers are collected as a transcript on the character's dossier.

## Catch up from a chapter

**Catch up from a chapter** reads a chapter you choose and lists **Suggested journal updates**: new characters, places and facts the chapter introduced. Press **Add** to keep a suggestion or **Skip** to ignore it. If nothing is new, the page says **Nothing new in that chapter. The journal is up to date.**
