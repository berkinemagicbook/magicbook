---
title: 'Spark'
description: 'Turn an idea, a what-if question or a picture into three premise cards, lock the lines you like, and choose the premise for your book.'
---

Spark turns a seed into the premise of the book. A premise is one sentence that says what the story is about.

## Pick a kind of seed

Spark starts with three kinds of seed:

- **An idea**: a sentence, a scene or a feeling, such as "a lighthouse keeper who has never seen the sea".
- **What if…**: a question the story answers, such as "what if the moon forgot how to rise?"
- **A picture**: a photo or a child's drawing. Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP file up to 10 MB. MagicBook checks the picture before it is used. You can add a note about it, such as who is in it.

Type your seed and press **Spark**. Spark shows **Shuffling the deck…** while it works.

## Choose a premise

Spark deals three premise cards. Each card has a title and a premise line.

- **Lock** any line to keep it.
- **Shuffle the rest** rewrites only the lines that are not locked.
- **Deal three premises** starts again from the seed.
- **This is my book** makes the chosen card the premise of the book. The premise is used by Shape, Plot and the manuscript.

<Note>
Locking a line keeps it when you shuffle. The title can be locked the same way with **Lock title**.
</Note>
