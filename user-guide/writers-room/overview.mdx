The Writer's Room is where authors plan, draft and revise a book. A book moves through five stages, shown as steps across the top of the page. You can go back to any stage at any time.

## The five stages

| Stage | What it is for | Hint on the step |
|---|---|---|
| **Spark** | Turn a seed into a premise: a sentence the whole book hangs on. | Find the idea |
| **Shape** | Decide the genre, tone, format, length and language, and write the back-cover blurb. | Genre, tone, length |
| **Cast & World** | Keep the Story Journal: characters, places, items, rules and groups. | People and places |
| **Plot** | Plan the chapters or pages along a story structure and tension curve. | The shape of the story |
| **Story Room** | See the whole book, arrange scenes, and write each scene in the manuscript. | Scenes and manuscript |

Picture books show **Pages** in place of Story Room, with the words and pictures for each page. See [Picture studio](/user-guide/picture-studio).

Each stage shows a tick when it is done, with a short note such as **Premise chosen** or **3 entries**.

## Work in any order

Most authors go down the list once, then move between stages as the book grows. A change in one stage can affect another. For example, a new chapter in Plot shows up as an empty scene in the Story Room. If a plan changes after scenes are written, the scene is marked as out of date so you can rewrite it.

## Story Journal

The **Story Journal** holds the facts the AI must keep straight: who people are, where places are, and the rules of the world. Open it from the journal button on any stage. It lists every entry by kind and is searchable by name or alias. Picking an entry opens it on **Cast & World**.

## Saving

Your edits save as you make them. The room waits about a second and a half after your last change, then saves everything together. In the manuscript the status shows **Saving…**, **Saved** or **Not saved**. Every AI change and a copy of your own writing are kept as versions, so nothing is lost.

## AI in the room

Each stage has AI buttons, and each one runs as a step with a cost in credits. The steps are described on [AI tools](/user-guide/ai-tools). Nothing changes your book unless you accept it. Suggestions can be used or dismissed.

## Formats and limits

A novel, chapter book, short story or picture book is chosen in Shape. The format cannot change once the book has chapters or pages. Length limits come from the site maximums and your plan. Shape shows **Up to N pages on your plan** for picture books, and the word limit for other formats.
