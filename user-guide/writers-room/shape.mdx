Shape decides what kind of book it is. Every later stage reads these settings.

## Genre

Drop a pin anywhere on the genre map. The position sets the blend of genres. Drag the pin, or use the arrow keys when the pin has focus.

## Tone

Four dials set the tone of the book. Move them to change how the book feels, from light to dark, quick to slow, and so on. The blurb updates when the tone changes.

## Format and audience

- **Format** is the kind of book: picture book, short story, chapter book or novel. A format your plan does not include shows **Not on your plan**. The format cannot change once the book has chapters or pages.
- **Audience** sets the age group the text is written for. Storyland and Family books are for ages 3 to 5 by default. Writer's Room books are for adults by default.
- **Length** is measured in pages for picture books, and in words for everything else. Shape shows the limit your plan allows, for example **Up to 24 pages on your plan**.
- **Language** is the language the book is written in.

The site's maximums and your plan's limits set the upper end. See [Book settings](/admin/book-settings).

## Back-cover blurb

The blurb is written from the premise, genre, tone and format. Press **Write the blurb** to write it, or **Write another** for a new version. If you change the book after the blurb was written, Shape shows **The book changed. Rewrite it** so you can update the blurb. You can also write your own.
