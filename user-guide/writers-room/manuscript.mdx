The manuscript is the page where a scene is written. It saves as you type, and every change is kept as a version.

## The page

The heading shows the chapter and the scene. The status shows **Saving…**, **Saved** or **Not saved**. Start typing in the empty page, or let the AI write the first draft.

## Write with the AI

- **Write this scene** drafts the scene from its plan, including its beats and who is in it.
- **Keep going** continues from where the text stops.
- **Write it again** replaces the draft with a new one. Use **Versions** to get back an earlier text.

While the AI works, the page shows **Writing the scene…** and **Inkling is writing**. When the draft is ready, you can keep it or put it back.

## Rewrite a selection

Select a passage and press **Rewrite the selection**. Type what to do, for example "make her angrier, cut the adjectives". Press **Go**, then check the new text before you keep it.

## Notes

Select text and add a note in the margin with **Add**. Notes are listed under **Notes**. **Hide done notes** hides the notes you have resolved. A note can be **Reopen**ed or marked **Done**.

## Check continuity

**Check continuity** compares the scene with the Story Journal. Its results appear as suggestions, and each one has **Use it** or **No thanks**. Nothing changes until you choose **Use it**.

## Beta readers

**Ask beta readers** asks simulated readers to react to the scene. Their reactions appear as a **heatmap** along the text, with reactions such as **gripped**, **moved**, **laughed**, **confused**, **bored** and **doubtful**.

## Versions

Versions are listed under **Versions**. Every AI change and a copy of your own writing every few minutes are kept. Press **Restore** to go back to an earlier version, or **Keep this one** to mark a version to keep. Versions are labelled **AI** or **You**.

## Your style

**Your style** learns how you write from your own text. **Match my style** makes new AI text follow it. **Teach it again** starts the style from your latest text.
