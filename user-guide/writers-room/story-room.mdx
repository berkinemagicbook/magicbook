title: 'Story Room'
description: 'See the whole book at three distances: the Book view of chapters, the Board corkboard with plot threads, and the Page view of one scene.'
---

The Story Room shows the whole book, and lets you zoom in on one scene. It has three views, chosen with the **Book**, **Board** and **Page** switch.

## Book view

Every chapter is a column of scene tiles, in order. A tile shows the scene's title and its status. Select a tile to open its details. **Add a chapter** adds one at the end, and **Drag to move the chapter** reorders them.

## Board view

The Board is a corkboard of scene cards. Drag a card between chapters to move a scene. **Plot threads** are strings across the board that link scenes. Add a thread with **+ new thread**, for example "the missing boat", and see every scene it touches. The **Key** explains the colours and the **Tension** marks.

## Page view

Page opens one scene's manuscript page. See [Manuscript](/user-guide/writers-room/manuscript). **Back to the board** returns to the Board.

## Scene details

Select a scene to open its details. Fields:

- **Title**
- **What happens**, the summary of the scene.
- **Beats**, one per line.
- **Point of view**, or **None**.
- **Length**, the **Target words** for the scene.
- **Status**, see below.
- **Who is in it**, from the Cast.
- **Where**, from the Places.

The status is one of **Empty**, **Planned**, **Drafting**, **Drafted**, **Needs update** or **Final**. A scene shows **Needs update** when its plan changed after it was written.

**Open the page** opens the scene in the manuscript. **Delete** removes the scene and its words, after you confirm. This cannot be undone.

## Write the scenes

- **Write the empty scenes of this chapter** writes every empty scene in one chapter.
- **Write them all** writes every empty scene in the book.
- While Inkling is writing, **Stop after this scene** stops after the scene that is being written.

A chapter with no scenes shows **Go to Plot**. Plot is where chapters are planned, and **Add a page** or **Add a chapter** are also available here.

When the book has no scenes, the Story Room shows **Nothing on the board yet.** and Inkling's note.
