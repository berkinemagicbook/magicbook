Narration reads a book aloud with a voice from ElevenLabs. Customers pick the narrator in the reader. The admin decides which voices are offered. See [Voices (admin)](/admin/voices).

## Choose a narrator

The narrator list shows:

- **Narrators** from the admin's list of switched-on voices.
- **Default narrator**, the voice used when you have not picked one.
- **For children**, the voices suited to Storyland and children's books. Children's books show only these.
- **Your voice**, when you have made a clone with the [Voice Cloning](/plugins/voice-cloning) plugin.

Press **Hear a sample** to hear a narrator before you choose.

## Record a book

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reader">
    Open the book and press **Read**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Narrate">
    Choose the narrator. Press **Record narration** to narrate the whole book, or **Record the rest** to narrate the chapters that are not yet recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Listen">
    Narrated chapters play in order. **Stop after this chapter** stops the book at the end of the current chapter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Narration is recorded in the background. The reader shows progress while it works. When recording is finished, the chapter plays from the saved audio.

## Cost

Narration costs credits per 1,000 characters. The admin sets the price on **AI → Voices**. A 300-page novel is about 600,000 characters. Narration also counts toward the **Narration minutes per month** on your plan. Plans without narration show **Narration is not part of your plan**.

## Inkling in Storyland

Inkling reads the story steps in Storyland. The admin can record Inkling's lines with a voice. Until then, Inkling uses the browser's own voice, so children hear a different voice in each browser.

## Voice input

Where the admin allows it, children can speak their story idea in Storyland with **Say it!**. Each recording is charged the admin's price per recording and is at most one minute long.
