---
title: 'Sharing and PDF'
description: 'Share a book with a private link that anyone can open, stop sharing, and download a screen-quality PDF.'
---

You can share a book with a private link, and download it as a PDF. Both work from the reader and from the bookshelf.

## Share a book

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share panel">
    In the reader, press **Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a link">
    Press **Make a link**. MagicBook creates a link that anyone can open without an account. Press **Copy link** to copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop sharing when you want">
    Press **Stop sharing**, and confirm. The link stops working for everyone at once. Making a new link later gives a different address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Shared books are never indexed by search engines. The link is a long random address, and only people with the link can open it.

Sharing is part of the **Share links** feature. It is included in the free tier by default. A plan can turn it off. See [Plans](/saas/plans).

## Download a PDF

Press **Download as PDF** in the reader, or **Download PDF** on the bookshelf. The PDF is made while you wait. A very long novel can take several seconds.

The PDF is screen quality. It uses the bundled Literata and Fredoka fonts. A language those fonts cannot show, such as Arabic, Chinese or Hindi, uses the DejaVu font instead.

<Note>
The PDF is made for reading on screens and at home printers. A print-ready file for a printing house is not part of the core product.
</Note>

If the download fails, check **System Status → PDF fonts**. See [PDF fonts](/advanced/pdf-fonts).
