The **Children** page holds the children that Family and Storyland stories are made for. Open it from **Books → Children** in the sidebar.

## Add a child

Click **Add a child**. The profile builder asks for:

- **Name** and **Age**.
- **How they look**: pick from the swatches for hair, skin and clothes. A live avatar updates as you choose.
- **What they love**: the things that should appear in their stories.
- **What stories should leave out**: subjects or fears to avoid.

Adding a child takes about a minute. Children's profiles are private to your account.

## See their stories

Each child's card shows how many of your books they appear in. Select a child to see their stories, most recently opened first. Select a book to open it.

## Use a child in a story

- In **Family**, pick the child on the recipe card under **Starring**. See [Family](/user-guide/family).
- In **Storyland**, a child can be the hero of a story, next to the characters. See [Storyland](/user-guide/storyland).

<Note>
If your plan does not include Family stories, the page says **Family stories are not included in your plan**. You can still add children and keep their profiles.
</Note>
