Family stories are personal. A parent picks the child, a place, a sidekick and a lesson, and MagicBook writes a story for that child tonight. Family books can be picture books, short stories or chapter books.

## Start from the recipe card

Start a Family book from the **Family** door on the bookshelf. The recipe card holds tonight's story. The left side has the choices, and the **Tonight's story** panel on the right fills in as you pick.

| Choice | What to pick |
|---|---|
| **Starring** | The children the story is about. Add a child from here if you have not added one yet. |
| **Where** | The place the story happens, such as **Grandma's farm**, or your own place. |
| **A sidekick** | A pet, a toy or a friend, such as a floppy toy rabbit. |
| **A little lesson** | What the story should show, such as being gentle with the cat. Shown through the story, never preached. |
| **Mood** | The feel of the story. |
| **Reading time** | How long it takes to read aloud. |
| **Something from today** | Optional. A sentence about the day, such as a first lost tooth. The story can use it. |

When the card is ready, press **Make the story**. Inkling writes every page for your child. Then you draw the pictures, one page at a time or all together, in the [picture studio](/user-guide/picture-studio).

The card is kept as a draft, so you can leave and come back to it.

## Use a photo for a closer likeness

If the site allows it and your plan includes **Child as the hero from a photo**, you can choose **Use a photo for a closer likeness**. Pick one photo of the child. MagicBook uses the photo once to draw the child's character sheet, then deletes it. Photos are never kept on the child's profile. A cleanup job removes any photo that was never used.

Only use a photo of a child you have permission to use.

## Plan and admin limits

A Family book needs the **Family** feature on your plan. Without it, the Bookshelf door shows **Not included in your plan**. Each book is also limited by the page or word maximums on your plan. See [Book settings](/admin/book-settings).
