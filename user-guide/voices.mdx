The **Voices** page lists every voice you can use to narrate a book. Open it from **Library → Voices** in the sidebar. The page shows the voices the site owner has switched on, plus your own cloned voices.

The voices page only appears when the site has an ElevenLabs key.

## Find a voice

- **Find a voice** searches the names.
- The tabs **All**, **Your voices**, **Default voices** and **Shortlist** narrow the list.
- **Sort voices** orders by **Recommended**, **Name A–Z** or **Most languages**.
- The filters **Style** (including **For children**), **Language**, **Gender**, **Age** and **Accent** narrow the list further. **Clear** removes them.

Searching and filtering happen in your browser, so the list updates at once.

## Hear a voice

Press **Play** on a voice to hear a preview. **Play all** plays the voices in order, and **Autoplay** plays the next voice when one ends. Keyboard shortcuts are shown on the page: Space plays or pauses, and the arrow keys move between voices.

## Languages

ElevenLabs decides which languages each voice speaks, and the list follows the model you pick. Open a voice to see **Languages on** the chosen model. Press a language to hear the voice speak it. If ElevenLabs has verified no languages for a voice on that model, the page says so.

## Shortlist

Press the star on a voice to keep it on your shortlist while you decide. **Shortlist** shows only the voices you starred.

## Use a voice

The voice details list where you can use the voice:

- **Book narration**: open a book, press **Read**, then **Narrate**. Children's books can use the voices marked **For children**.
- **Audiobook Studio**: cast the voice as the narrator or a character. See [Audiobook Studio](/plugins/audiobook-studio).
- **Storyland**: the voice reads Storyland books aloud to children.

To make a voice of your own, use **Clone your own voice** on the page. See [Voice Cloning](/plugins/voice-cloning).
