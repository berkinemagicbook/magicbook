Storyland is made for children. They make a picture book by tapping pictures, and nobody has to type. Inkling the owl reads every question and every choice aloud.

Open Storyland from the **Storyland home** button on the bookshelf, or go to `/app/user/storyland`. Parents can lock a device to Storyland with [kid mode](/user-guide/kid-mode).

## The home page

The home page shows the child's books as big covers. **Make a new story** starts a book. Tap **Hear Inkling** to hear Inkling say hello and explain what to do.

## Make a story

A story takes five picks, one after another. Each pick is made by tapping a picture.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hero">
    Choose who the story is about. The hero can be one of the characters, or a child from the family's Children list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friend">
    Choose a friend to come along, or **Just me!**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place">
    Choose where the story happens. The sky and colours of the page change to match the place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adventure">
    Choose what goes wrong or what happens, the problem that the story solves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stickers">
    Add up to three stickers to the story.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two extra options help along the way:

- **Roll the dice for a surprise story** fills every pick at random. The dice spins across the stops and then lands on a choice.
- **Say it!** lets the child tell Inkling anything else about the story out loud. It appears when the site has voice input on. See [Voices](/admin/voices).

When the picks are done, press **Make my book!**. Inkling writes every page and draws it while the screen shows **Writing the words…**. The book then opens with **Read my book!**. **Make another story** starts over.

## When something goes wrong

If a step fails, the screen shows **Oh! Inkling got stuck.** and asks a grown-up to help. Ask a parent to open the book from the bookshelf, or to start a new story. Failed steps are not charged. See [AI tools](/user-guide/ai-tools).

## Safety

Children's text is checked for safety before it is shown, and it is never streamed word by word. Admins set the check for each audience on **AI → Safety**. See [Safety](/admin/safety).
