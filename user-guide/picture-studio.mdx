title: 'Picture studio'
description: 'Write, arrange and draw the pages and cover of a picture book, in one art style, with the same characters on every page.'
---

The picture studio makes and draws picture books. It is used by picture books in every mode: Storyland, Family and the Writer's Room.

## Start from a story idea

An empty picture book starts from a story idea. Describe the story in a sentence, such as "a shy hedgehog who wants to join the moonlight parade". Then choose who is in the story. You can also let Inkling invent the cast.

Press **Write the pages**. Inkling writes every page, with:

- the words on the page,
- a note for what the picture shows,
- who is in the picture.

You can change every word and picture afterwards. The button shows the approximate credit cost before you press it.

## Draw the pages

Pages sit in a tray along the bottom. Select a page to edit it, or use the arrow keys to turn pages.

- **Draw this page** draws the picture for the selected page.
- **Redraw this page** draws it again. The earlier pictures stay in **Earlier pictures**, and **Use this picture** brings one back.
- **Draw the cover** draws the front cover, and **Draw a new cover** redraws it.

Every picture is drawn from the characters' character sheets, so the same character looks the same from page to page. Pages can be dragged into a new order in the tray.

## Page details

Each page has these fields:

- **Words on the page**, with the **placement** of the words, such as where they sit on the picture.
- **What the picture shows**.
- **Who is in the picture**, from the cast.

Use **Add a page after this one** to add a page, or **Remove page** to delete one. Removing a page also removes its pictures.

## Art style

Choose an art style for the whole book. Styles come from the admin's list, such as watercolour or crayon. See [Art styles](/admin/art-styles).

## Requirements

Drawing needs an image model that the admin has switched on, and at least one that can use character sheets. See [Image models](/admin/image-models). If no model is available, the page says so.
