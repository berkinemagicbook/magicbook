---
title: 'Kid mode'
description: 'Lock the app into Storyland for children, and leave kid mode with the grown-up PIN.'
---

Kid mode keeps a child inside Storyland. In kid mode, only Storyland and the reader stay open. Leaving kid mode needs the grown-up PIN.

## Turn kid mode on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the grown-ups' door">
    Use the **Grown-ups only** link in Storyland to reach the grown-ups' door.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the PIN">
    The first time, you set a four-digit PIN and enter it again to confirm. Choose a PIN the child does not know.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start kid mode">
    Press **Start kid mode**. Press **Not now** to stay in the normal app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Leave kid mode

Turning kid mode off always asks for the PIN. Enter the four digits on the pad. The **Delete** button removes a digit.

After five wrong guesses, the PIN pad waits one minute before it accepts another try.

## Forgot the PIN

Press **Forgot the PIN?** and follow the instructions to sign out and sign in again with the parent's account. Then set a new PIN when you start kid mode again.

<Note>
Kid mode does not create a separate account. The child's books, pictures and voices stay on the parent's account.
</Note>
