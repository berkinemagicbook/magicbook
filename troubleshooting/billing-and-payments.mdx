---
title: 'Billing and payments problems'
description: 'Fix a missing payment option, a failed checkout, a webhook that rejects payments, bank transfers that stay pending, and renewals that fail.'
---

Billing problems come from one of three places: the gateway's settings, the webhook that tells MagicBook a payment succeeded, or the plan's own setup. Start with the order's status on [Orders](/saas/orders).

## No payment option at checkout

- The gateway's **Status** is off, or the gateway has no saved keys. Turn it on and save. See [Payment gateways](/gateways/overview).
- The plan's currency is not one the gateway accepts.
- The plan is **Closed** or **Hidden** on [Plans](/saas/plans).

## Checkout fails

Read the message the customer sees, and the latest lines of `storage/logs/laravel.log`. Common causes:

- A wrong secret key, or the test key used in live mode. Check the mode on the gateway page.
- The gateway could not create the plan for a monthly or yearly subscription. The log names the reason.

## Payment done, but the plan is not active

The payment is recorded when the customer returns from the gateway, and again when the gateway's webhook arrives. If both fail, the order stays **Pending**.

- Check that the webhook URL on the gateway matches the URL on its settings page.
- Check that the **Webhook Signing Secret** belongs to the mode you use.
- Open the order and approve it if it is a bank transfer.

## Webhook rejected

Gateways show failed deliveries with the response. A response of `invalid signature` or `webhook secret missing` means the signing secret is wrong or empty. Copy the secret from the gateway's webhook settings into the gateway page, and save.

## Bank transfer stays pending

A bank transfer is confirmed by hand. Check your bank account, then open the order and approve it. If the customer uploaded a proof of payment, check it against the transfer first. See [Orders](/saas/orders).

## Renewal failed

A renewal that fails shows as **Failed** on the order. Ask the customer to check their payment method on **Billing**. A subscription the gateway cancelled shows as **Cancelled**.

## Credits did not arrive

Check the order's status. Credits are added when the order is **Completed**. For a pending bank transfer, approve the order. For a completed order, check that the plan includes the credits. See [Plans](/saas/plans).
