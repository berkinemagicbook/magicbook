## Do I need a paid AI account?

Yes. MagicBook calls each AI provider with your own key, so the provider bills you for the work. The SaaS plugin lets you charge customers for the same work.

## Which AI key do I need first?

Start with one text provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or xAI) and one image provider. Add OpenAI as well, because its safety check is free and children's books need it. Add ElevenLabs only if you want narration. See [Requirements](/getting-started/requirements).

## Can children use MagicBook without a parent?

Storyland is made for children, but a parent sets it up. Parents can lock the device to Storyland with [kid mode](/user-guide/kid-mode), and a parent PIN is needed to leave it.

## Who owns the books customers make?

Books belong to the customer's account and are stored on your server. Your terms should say how customers may use what they make. The privacy policy and terms are edited on [Website content](/admin/website-content).

## Is a child's photo kept?

No. A photo is used once to draw the child's character sheet, then deleted. The site owner can turn the photo option off on [Book settings](/admin/book-settings).

## Can I change a plan after customers have bought it?

Yes. Changing the price creates a new price at the gateway for the next purchase. Existing subscriptions keep their price. See [Plans](/saas/plans).

## Can I use MagicBook without the SaaS plugin?

Yes. The core works without it. Customers then have no plans or checkout, and the site is a free service for everyone.

## Does MagicBook need ffmpeg?

No. Only the optional Audiobook Studio and Voice Cloning plugins use it. See [ffmpeg](/advanced/ffmpeg).

## Where do I see what a customer's AI step cost?

Open **AI → Generation activity**, or the customer's page under **Accounts → Users**. See [Generation activity](/admin/generation-activity).

## Can customers print their books?

Yes, as a screen-quality PDF. The PDF is made for reading and home printing, and is not a print-ready file for a printing house. See [Sharing and PDF](/user-guide/sharing-and-pdf).

## Is there a demo I can try?

Run the demo seeder described in the [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) to add sample books to an admin account.
