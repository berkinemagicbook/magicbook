---
title: 'Narration and PDF problems'
description: 'Fix a missing Narrate button, narration that will not start, audio that does not play, and PDF downloads that fail.'
---

Narration and PDF downloads are the two features that depend on the most outside pieces: an ElevenLabs key, a plan that includes them, the background work, and fonts on the server.

## No Narrate button

The **Narrate** button is hidden until all of these are true:

- An ElevenLabs key is saved on [AI providers](/admin/ai-providers).
- At least one voice is switched on in [Voices](/admin/voices).
- The customer's plan includes **Narration**.

The reader shows which one is missing. If it says **Narration is not part of your plan.**, the plan needs narration. See [Plans](/saas/plans).

## Narration does not start

- The customer has no credits left for the narration price. The reader and the account show the balance.
- The cron job is not running. Narration is recorded in the background. See [Scheduler and queue problems](/troubleshooting/scheduler-and-queue).
- The ElevenLabs key or the voice is no longer valid. Check the step's error on [Generation activity](/admin/generation-activity).

## Audio does not play

- Browsers block sound until the reader presses a button. Press **Hear a sample** or **Narrate** first.
- Check the device volume and the browser's sound settings for the site.
- Reload the page. If one chapter still does not play, ask the customer to record it again with **Record narration**.

## Narrator missing from the list

A voice appears only when the admin has switched it on. Children's books show only the voices marked **For children**.

## PDF download fails

- Open **Platform → System Status** and check **PDF fonts**. See [PDF fonts](/advanced/pdf-fonts).
- The folder `storage/fonts` must be writable by the web server.
- A very long book can take longer than the server allows. Raise the PHP `max_execution_time` for the site, or ask the customer to try a shorter book.
- The download is limited to ten per minute for each customer. A customer who tries too often sees a short wait.

## Share link does not open

Check that **Share** shows a link and that the book is still shared. Press **Stop sharing** and then **Make a link** again to create a new one. See [Sharing and PDF](/user-guide/sharing-and-pdf).
