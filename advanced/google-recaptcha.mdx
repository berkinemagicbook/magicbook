---
title: 'Google reCAPTCHA'
description: 'Protect the public forms from bots with Google reCAPTCHA v3, using a site key and a secret key.'
---

Google reCAPTCHA v3 checks visitors in the background and scores how likely they are to be a bot. MagicBook uses it on public forms, such as sign-up and the contact form.

## Create the keys

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the site">
    Open the [reCAPTCHA admin console](https://www.google.com/recaptcha/admin) and register a new site. Choose **reCAPTCHA v3**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Add the domain your site uses, such as `magicbook.example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the keys">
    Copy the **site key** and the **secret key**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save them in MagicBook

Open **Backend Settings → Global Settings**, turn on **Enable Google reCaptcha v3**, paste the **Google reCaptcha Site Key** and the **Google reCaptcha Secret Key**, then press **Save**.

<Note>
The site key is visible in the page code, which is normal. Keep the secret key private.
</Note>
