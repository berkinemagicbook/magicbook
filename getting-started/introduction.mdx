## What MagicBook is

MagicBook is a self-hosted web application that writes books with AI. You install it on your own server, save the AI provider keys you pay for, and your customers make books in your site. Every page of the story (text, pictures and narration) is written and drawn by the AI providers you choose.

It is built on Laravel 13 and Livewire 4. It needs PHP 8.3 or newer, a MySQL or MariaDB database and one cron job, so it runs on ordinary shared hosting. The core does not need ffmpeg, a Node process or a queue supervisor.

## The three modes

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Storyland" icon="shapes">
    Children make a picture book by tapping pictures. Inkling the owl reads every step aloud, so nobody has to type.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Family" icon="heart">
    Parents make a personal bedtime story that stars their own child, starting from a recipe card.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Writer's Room" icon="pen-nib">
    Authors plan, draft and revise a novel, a chapter book or a short story, with a Story Journal for characters and places.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Every mode ends in a book on the shelf. A book can be read on the screen, shared by private link, downloaded as a PDF and, with narration, read aloud.

## Who uses MagicBook

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="user-shield">
    The site owner. The admin installs MagicBook, saves the AI keys, chooses which models write and draw, sets the limits for each book format, manages users and, with the SaaS plugin, sells plans.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customers" icon="book-open">
    People who register on your site. They make books in one of the three modes, read them, share them, and keep their children and voices in their library.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
