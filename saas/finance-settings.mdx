**Finance → Finance Settings** holds the money settings for the whole site.

## General

- **Default Currency**: the currency used for new plans and prices shown on the pricing page. Each plan can still use its own currency.
- **Default Tax %**: the tax added to orders by default. Set it to `0` if you do not charge tax.
- **Billing Information**: when on, customers must fill in their billing details at checkout before they pay.
- **Decimal Points in Prices**: when on, prices show the cents or pence after the main amount.

Press **Save** to keep the changes.

<Note>
The currency of a payment must be one your payment gateway accepts. Check the gateway's supported currencies before you change the default. See [Payment gateways](/gateways/overview).
</Note>
