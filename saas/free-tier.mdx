---
title: 'Free tier'
description: 'Set the features and limits that apply to customers who do not have a plan while billing is on.'
---

Customers without a plan use the free tier. Set it on **Finance → Free tier**.

The page holds the limits for users without a plan, and the features they get. Each limit and feature uses the same options as a plan. See [Plans](/saas/plans).

## Limits

The free tier has its own value for each limit. The most common one is **Media storage**: how much space the media library of a free user may use.

## Features

By default the free tier includes:

- **Storyland**
- **Family**
- **Writer's Room**
- **Share links**

Tick the features that free users may use, and leave the others off.

<Note>
The free tier only applies while billing is on. When the SaaS plugin is switched off, every customer uses the site without plan limits.
</Note>

Press **Save free tier** to keep the changes.
