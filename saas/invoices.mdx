**Finance → Invoice Settings** holds the company details that appear on every invoice.

## Company information

Fill in:

- **Company Name**
- **Company Website**
- **Business Address**, with **City**, **State**, **Postal Code** and **Country**
- **Phone Number**
- **VAT Number**, if you are registered for VAT

Press **Save** to keep the details.

## Download invoices

- Admins download an invoice from the order. See [Orders](/saas/orders).
- Customers download their own invoices from **Billing** and from their profile under **Recent billing**. See [Billing](/account/billing).

Check the first invoice before you sell, because it is the document your customers keep for their records.
