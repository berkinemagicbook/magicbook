A plan decides what a customer can do and how much. Create plans under **Finance → Pricing Plans**. Press **New plan** to start one, or select a plan to edit it.

## General

- **Plan name**: shown on the pricing page, at checkout and on the billing page.
- **Description**: a short line shown with the plan.
- **Status**: **Active**, **Closed** or **Hidden**. Set it to control whether new customers can buy the plan.
- **Featured**: highlights the plan above the others on the pricing page.

## Pricing

- **Plan type**: **Monthly**, **Yearly**, **Lifetime** or **Prepaid**. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically. Lifetime and prepaid plans are one-time payments. See [Prepaid plans](/saas/prepaid-plans).
- **Price** and **Currency**: what the customer pays each billing cycle, or once for lifetime and prepaid plans.
- **Free plan**: customers can sign up to it without paying.
- **Free trial days**: how long before the first charge. Leave it blank for no trial.

## Credits

- The number of credits a subscriber gets.
- Credits for monthly, yearly and lifetime plans renew each month. Unused monthly credits do not carry over.
- Prepaid plan credits do not renew, and stay until spent.
- Use `-1` to give unlimited credits.

## Limits

Each limit is a number the plan allows. The site maximums always apply on top. See [Book settings](/admin/book-settings).

| Limit | What it caps |
|---|---|
| **New books per month** | Books a customer may start in a month. |
| **Pages per picture book** | Pages in one picture book. |
| **Words per chapter book or short story** | Words in one chapter book or short story. |
| **Words per novel** | Words in one novel. |
| **Illustrations per month** | Pictures drawn in a month. |
| **Narration minutes per month** | Minutes of narration in a month. |
| **Media storage** | Space the media library may use, in MB. |

## What's included

Tick the features the plan includes:

- **Storyland**, **Family** and **Writer's Room**, the three modes.
- **Child as the hero from a photo**, for a photo in a Family book.
- **Narration**, and **PDF download** and **Share links**.
- **Premium illustration models**, for the image models marked premium.
- **Audiobook Studio** and **Voice Cloning**, when those plugins are installed.

A feature that a customer's plan does not include is shown as locked.

## Custom features

Custom features are text perks listed on the pricing page, such as "Priority support". They do not grant anything in the app. Use them for information only.

<Warning>
Changing a plan's price, currency or type creates a new price at the payment gateway the next time it is bought. Existing subscriptions keep their old price. See [Stripe](/gateways/stripe) for how each gateway handles this.
</Warning>
