An order is one payment: a new plan, a renewal, a credit purchase or a top-up. Open **Finance → Orders** to see them all.

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Completed** | The payment succeeded, and the plan or credits were given. |
| **Pending** | The payment has not arrived yet. Bank transfers stay pending until you approve them. |
| **Failed** | The gateway rejected the payment. |
| **Cancelled** | The customer or the admin cancelled the order. |
| **Declined** | The payment was declined by the bank or the gateway. |

## Approve a bank transfer

Bank transfer orders wait for you to confirm the money arrived. The admin dashboard lists them under **Bank transfers** and shows **Proof uploaded** when the customer has sent a receipt.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    Select the pending order on **Orders**, or from the admin dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the proof">
    Open the **proof of payment** the customer uploaded, if they did, and check the amount and reference against your bank statement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Approve the order. The plan and its credits are given to the customer straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View an order

The order page shows the customer, the items, the amount and tax, the gateway used and the status. From here you can download the invoice, or open the customer's payment proof.

## Invoices

Press **Invoice** to download the order's PDF invoice. The invoice uses the company details set on [Invoices](/saas/invoices). Customers can download their own invoices from **Billing**.
