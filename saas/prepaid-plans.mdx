---
title: 'Prepaid plans'
description: 'Sell one-time prepaid plans whose credits do not renew and stay until spent.'
---

A **Prepaid** plan is a one-time purchase of credits. It does not renew. The customer pays once, receives the plan's credits, and keeps them until they spend them.

## When to use one

- Sell a bundle of credits for customers who want to pay once.
- Offer a top-up for customers who run out of their monthly allowance.

## Set up a prepaid plan

On the plan form, choose **Plan type** **Prepaid**. Then set the price, the currency and the credits.

- Prepaid credits are not renewed. The plan form says "valid until depleted".
- The plan's features and limits work like any other plan. See [Plans](/saas/plans).
- Use `-1` for credits only if the plan is meant to be unlimited.

## Customers

A customer who bought a prepaid plan sees the credits on the **Credits** page, under **Credit packs**. The credits stay available until spent. See [Credits](/account/credits).
