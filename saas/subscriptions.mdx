description: 'See and manage customer subscriptions to monthly and yearly plans, and what happens when one is cancelled or renews.'
---

A subscription links a customer to a plan that renews. Open **Finance → Subscriptions** to see them. The page shows the total number of subscribers.

## Statuses

A subscription can be **Active**, **Cancelled**, **Ended** or **Inactive**. Only an **Active** subscription gives the plan's features and credits.

## View a subscription

Select a subscription to open its page. It shows the customer, the plan and its status, and the renewal date.

## Cancel

A customer cancels from **Billing**. An admin can also cancel a subscription from its page. For gateways that take cancellations from MagicBook, the subscription is cancelled at the gateway too, so the customer is not charged again. See the gateway's own page under [Payment gateways](/gateways/overview).

When a subscription ends, the customer falls back to the free tier. Their books and files stay on their account.

## Renewals

Each renewal creates an order at the plan's price. A renewal that fails shows in [Orders](/saas/orders) with the status **Failed**. See [Billing and payments problems](/troubleshooting/billing-and-payments).
