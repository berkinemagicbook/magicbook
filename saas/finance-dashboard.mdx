---

**Finance → Finance Dashboard** shows how the business is doing. It is the first finance page to open after a sale.

## Headline figures

- **Revenue**, with **Lifetime** and **This month** totals.
- **Recurring**, the monthly recurring revenue from monthly and yearly plans.
- **Active Subscriptions**, the number of subscribers now.
- **Churn**, the share of subscribers who cancelled.

## Charts and breakdowns

- **Revenue Overview** shows monthly revenue against cost.
- **Revenue by Gateway** shows which payment method brings in the most.
- **Order Status** shows the share of **Completed**, **Pending**, **Failed**, **Cancelled** and **Declined** orders over all time.
- **Active Plan Distribution** shows how many subscribers are on each plan.
- **Revenue by Plan** shows which plans earn the most.

## Lists

- **Recent Orders** shows the latest transactions. **View All** opens [Orders](/saas/orders).
- **Top Customers** ranks customers by total spend, with their order count.
- **Top Plans** ranks plans by revenue.
