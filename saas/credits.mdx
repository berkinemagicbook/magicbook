Credits pay for AI work. Customers spend them when they write, draw, narrate or record a voice. This page explains where credits come from as an admin. For the customer's view, see [Credits](/account/credits).

## Where credits come from

- **Sign-up**: each new account receives the **Default Registration Credits** set on [General settings](/admin/general-settings).
- **Plan allowance**: a monthly, yearly or lifetime plan gives credits each month. Unused credits do not carry over.
- **Prepaid plans**: a one-time purchase gives credits that stay until spent. See [Prepaid plans](/saas/prepaid-plans).
- **Admin adjustments**: you can see and release holds on a user's page under **Accounts → Users**. See [Users](/admin/users).

## How credits are spent

Each AI step holds its estimated cost when it starts and charges the actual cost when it finishes. Holds that never finish are released by the background work. See [Cron and queue](/installation/cron-and-queue).

The order of spending is the plan allowance first, then credit packs. Accounts with unlimited credits are not charged.

## Monthly renewal

Every hour, the background work renews the allowance for plans that are due. A customer whose monthly plan renewed gets the new allowance on the renewal date. The old allowance does not carry over.

## Costs

The price of each AI step is set on the model pages: [Text models](/admin/text-models), [Image models](/admin/image-models), [Voices](/admin/voices). Set them so that the credits you sell cover your AI provider bill.
