---
title: 'SaaS overview'
description: 'How the SaaS plugin adds plans, credits, payments, invoices, subscriptions and referrals to MagicBook.'
---

The SaaS plugin turns MagicBook into a paid service. It adds plans, a free tier, credits, subscriptions, payment gateways, invoices and a referral program. Without it, MagicBook has no billing, so every customer uses the site without plan limits.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the plugin">
    Open **Platform → Plugins**, find the SaaS plugin and press **Install**. See [SaaS plugin](/plugins/saas).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it">
    Open **Platform → General Settings → Plugins**, open the **SaaS Business** card and turn on **Enable SaaS Business**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the money">
    Set the currency and tax on [Finance settings](/saas/finance-settings), the company details on [Invoices](/saas/invoices), and at least one payment gateway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the plans">
    Create the plans on [Plans](/saas/plans) and the free tier on [Free tier](/saas/free-tier).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes for customers

- The sidebar gains a **Plans** section with **Billing** and **Referrals**.
- A customer with a plan gets the features and limits of that plan.
- A customer without a plan gets the free tier.
- Every credit spent is checked against the balance, including the plan allowance and credit packs. See [Credits](/account/credits).

## What changes for admins

The sidebar gains the **Finance** section: **Finance Dashboard**, **Pricing Plans**, **Free tier**, **Orders**, **Subscriptions**, **Invoice Settings**, **Payment Gateways** and **Finance Settings**. It also gains an **Affiliates** section for the referral program.
