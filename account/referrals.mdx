The referral program pays you when people you bring in buy a plan. Open **Plans → Referrals** in the sidebar.

<Note>
The referral program is part of the SaaS plugin. It appears only when the site has billing turned on.
</Note>

## Share your link

The page shows your **share link** and your **referral ID**. Copy the link and share it. Anyone who signs up through it is counted as your referral.

## Track your results

- **Lifetime stats** show the customers you referred and the total commissions earned.
- The **Referred customers** list shows who signed up through your link, and whether they bought a plan.
- The **available to withdraw** balance is the commissions earned, less payouts already requested or paid.

## Request a payout

Keep your payout details up to date on the page, such as a PayPal email or bank details. When your available balance reaches the threshold the site owner has set, use the payout request option on the page. The request appears in your payout history, with its status, until it is paid.

See [Affiliates](/saas/affiliates) for how the site owner handles payouts.
