---

**Plans → Billing** shows the plans and your current plan. The page is titled **Pick the plan that fits your workflow**.

<Note>
Billing appears only when the site has the SaaS plugin turned on.
</Note>

## Choose a plan

Each plan shows its price, its type (**Monthly**, **Yearly**, **Lifetime** or **Prepaid**), its credits and the features it includes. Press the plan's button to go to checkout. The payment page is hosted by the payment provider the site uses.

Upgrades, downgrades and cancellations take effect straight away. Each change is shown on the billing page.

## Your current plan

**Your current plan** shows the plan name, the credits remaining and the renewal date. When you have no subscription, it shows **No active subscription.**

## Cancel a subscription

Press **Cancel subscription** and confirm. The page shows **Cancelling…** while the cancellation is saved.

## Billing history

The history lists every order with its **Date**, **Plan**, **Amount** and **Status**. Press **Invoice** or **Download** to get the invoice as a PDF. The invoice shows the company details of the site.

## Bank transfer

If the site accepts bank transfers, the checkout shows the bank details and a reference to quote. After you transfer the money, upload the receipt under **Recent billing** in your profile, with **Upload proof of payment**. The order is confirmed once the site owner approves it. See [Profile and security](/account/profile-and-security).

## Problems with a payment

See [Billing and payments problems](/troubleshooting/billing-and-payments). If the problem is not listed there, open a ticket under [Support](/account/support) and include the order reference.
