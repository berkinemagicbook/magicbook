---
title: 'Notifications'
description: 'Read the notifications inbox for updates about your account and billing, and mark them as read.'
---

The **Notifications** page is your inbox. It holds updates about your account and your billing. Open it from the **Notifications** item in the sidebar. The bell item shows how many messages are unread.

## Read messages

- **All** shows every message.
- **Unread** shows only the messages you have not read yet.
- **Mark all as read** marks every message as read at once.

When nothing is waiting, the page says **You are all caught up**. When the inbox has never had a message, it says **Nothing here yet**.
