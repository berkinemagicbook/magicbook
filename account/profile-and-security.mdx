---
title: 'Profile and security'
description: 'Update your profile and contact details, change your password, verify your email and turn on two-factor authentication.'
---

Open your profile from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Profile**. The page is titled **My Profile**.

## Profile photo

Press **Change profile photo**, choose **Upload new photo**, then **Save photo**.

## Personal information

Under **Identity**, change your **Full name** and **Email address**. Under **Company & contact**, you can add your company, phone number, website and address. Press **Save changes** to save them.

If your email address is not verified, the page shows **Your email address is unverified.** Press **Re-send verification email** to get a new link. 

## Password

Open **Settings → Password** to change your password. Use a long password you do not use anywhere else.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication asks for a one-time PIN each time you sign in, in addition to your password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the security settings">
    Open **Settings → Two-factor** and read the page title, **Two-Factor Authentication**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it">
    Press **Enable 2FA**. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, or press **enter the code manually** and type the key in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Enter the six-digit code from the app and press **Confirm**. Keep the recovery codes somewhere safe. They let you sign in if you lose the device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To turn it off, press **Disable 2FA**. The status changes to **Disabled**.

## Recent billing

The profile page also lists your latest orders under **Recent billing**, with a **Download invoice** link for each. For bank transfers, use **Upload proof of payment** to send the transfer receipt. See [Billing](/account/billing).

## Your plan

The profile shows your current plan, its renewal date and the **Manage subscription** button. When you have no plan, it shows **Free plan**.
