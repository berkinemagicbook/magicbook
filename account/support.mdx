If you need help, open a ticket under **Help → Support** in the sidebar. The page is titled **Help & Support**, and every conversation with the team stays in your list of tickets.

## Open a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press New Ticket">
    Press **New Ticket** or **Create a Ticket**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Fill in:

    - **Subject**: a short summary.
    - **Category**: **Technical**, **Billing**, **Account**, **General** or **Feature Request**.
    - **Priority**: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Urgent**. Choose **Urgent** only when you cannot work at all.
    - **Message**: what happened, what you expected, and the steps that led to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a file (optional)">
    Press **Attach File** to add a JPG, JPEG or PNG screenshot, up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Press **Submit Ticket**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow a ticket

The list shows your tickets with their status: **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed**. Use **Search tickets...** to find one, or filter by status. Select a ticket to read the replies and answer.

<Note>
Include the book's title and the time of the problem. For a failed AI step, include the reason shown in the **AI activity** card.
</Note>
