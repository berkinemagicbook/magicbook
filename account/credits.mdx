Credits pay for AI work: writing, pictures, narration and voice input. Open **Settings → Credits** to see your balance and history.

## Where credits come from

- **Plan allowance**: the credits your plan gives you each month. Spent first. Topped up when your plan renews. Unused allowance does not carry over to the next month.
- **Credit packs**: credits bought separately. They never expire.

Some plans have unlimited credits. The page then shows **Unlimited**, and runs are covered by them.

## The credits page

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Available** | Your balance: the plan allowance and credit packs you can spend now. |
| **On hold** | Credits reserved for work that is still running. |
| **This month** | How much you spent and added this month. |
| **Credits spent each day** | A chart of the last 30 days. |
| **History** | Every credit added or spent. Filter by **All**, **Spent** or **Added**. |

A history entry marked **Not charged** is work that was blocked or failed. It holds nothing.

## Holds

When an AI step starts, its estimated cost is put on hold. When the step finishes, the actual cost is charged and the rest is released. If the step fails or stalls, the whole hold is released. See [AI tools](/user-guide/ai-tools).

## Buy credits

Choose **Get credits** on Home, or open **Plans → Billing**. The purchase options depend on the plans and credit packs the site offers. See [Billing](/account/billing).
