## Appearance

Open **Settings → Appearance** to choose how the app looks:

- **Light**
- **Dark**
- **System**, which follows your device's setting


## Language

The **account menu** at the bottom of the sidebar has a language choice. It lists the languages the site owner has turned on in the language manager. Choose one and the page reloads in that language. Your choice is remembered.

Books keep the language they were written in. The language choice changes only the app's buttons, labels and messages. See [Languages](/admin/languages).
