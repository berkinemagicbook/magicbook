---
title: 'LinkedIn login'
description: 'Let customers sign in with their LinkedIn account by creating a LinkedIn app and saving its keys in MagicBook.'
---

LinkedIn login lets customers sign in with their LinkedIn account.

## Create the LinkedIn app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an app">
    Open the [LinkedIn Developer portal](https://www.linkedin.com/developers/) and create an app. Link it to a company page, which LinkedIn requires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URL">
    In the app's **Auth** settings, add the **Redirect URL** shown on **Backend Settings → Auth Settings → LinkedIn** to the authorised redirect URLs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request sign-in access">
    In the app's products, request the sign-in product so that people can sign in with LinkedIn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the keys">
    Copy the **Client ID** and the **Client Secret**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save the keys in MagicBook

Open **Backend Settings → Auth Settings**, turn on **Enable LinkedIn Login**, paste the **LinkedIn API Key** (the Client ID) and the **LinkedIn API Secret Key** (the Client Secret), then press **Save**.
