---
title: 'Facebook login'
description: 'Let customers sign in with Facebook by creating a Facebook app and saving its keys in MagicBook.'
---

Facebook login lets customers sign in with their Facebook account.

## Create the Facebook app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an app">
    Open the [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/) site, create an app and add the **Facebook Login** product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
    Copy the **Redirect URL** from **Backend Settings → Auth Settings → Facebook**. Add it to the app's valid OAuth redirect URIs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the keys">
    Copy the app ID and the app secret.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save the keys in MagicBook

Open **Backend Settings → Auth Settings**, turn on **Enable Facebook Login**, paste the **Facebook API Key** (the app ID) and the **Facebook API Secret Key** (the app secret), then press **Save**.

<Note>
Keep the app in development mode until you have tested the sign-in with a second account. Switch it to live when you are ready for customers.
</Note>
