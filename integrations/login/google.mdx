---
title: 'Google login'
description: 'Let customers sign in with their Google account by creating a Google OAuth client and saving its keys in MagicBook.'
---

Google login lets customers sign in with their Google account.

## Create the Google client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Google Cloud Console">
    Go to the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/), choose or create a project, and open the OAuth consent screen. Fill it in with your site name and contact email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the credentials">
    Create an **OAuth client ID** for a web application. Copy the **Redirect URL** from **Backend Settings → Auth Settings → Google** and add it as an authorised redirect URI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the keys">
    Copy the **Client ID** and the **Client Secret**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save the keys in MagicBook

Open **Backend Settings → Auth Settings**, turn on **Login via Social (oAuth)** and **Enable Google Login**, paste the **Google Client ID** and **Google Client Secret**, then press **Save**. The sign-in page then shows a Google button.

<Note>
The redirect URI must match the Redirect URL exactly, including the `https://` and the domain. Google refuses to sign people in if they differ.
</Note>
