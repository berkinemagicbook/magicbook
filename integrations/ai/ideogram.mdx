---
title: 'Ideogram'
description: 'Connect Ideogram for picture models that are strong at covers and lettering.'
---

Ideogram provides image models that handle lettering well. MagicBook uses them for pictures, and they suit book covers that carry a title.

## Get a key

Follow the setup steps in the [Ideogram API guide](https://developer.ideogram.ai/ideogram-api/api-setup) to create a key.

## Add it to MagicBook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers">
    Go to **AI → AI Providers** and find the **Ideogram** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Press **Add key**, paste the key and press **Save and test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the models">
    Enable the Ideogram models on [Image models](/admin/image-models). Pick them for the **Cover** job if covers should carry lettering.
  </Step>
</Steps>
