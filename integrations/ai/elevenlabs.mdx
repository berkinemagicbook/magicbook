ElevenLabs provides the voices MagicBook uses for narration, for voice input, and for reading Storyland aloud. Voice Cloning and Audiobook Studio also use ElevenLabs.

## Get a key

Create a key in your [ElevenLabs API keys](https://elevenlabs.io/app/settings/api-keys) page.

## Add it to MagicBook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers">
    Go to **AI → AI Providers** and find the **ElevenLabs** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Press **Add key**, paste the key and press **Save and test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load the voices">
    Open **AI → Voices**, press **Get voices from ElevenLabs**, and switch on the narrators you want to offer. See [Voices](/admin/voices).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Narration, the Voices library and the voice input option appear only after the key is saved and at least one voice is switched on.

## Costs

Narration costs credits per 1,000 characters, and voice input costs credits per recording. The prices are set on the Voices page. ElevenLabs charges your account for each character it reads, so set the customer price above your ElevenLabs cost.

## Voice Cloning

Each cloned voice takes one custom voice slot on your ElevenLabs account. See [Voice Cloning](/plugins/voice-cloning).
