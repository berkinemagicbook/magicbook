---
title: 'FLUX (Black Forest Labs)'
description: 'Connect Black Forest Labs FLUX for illustrations, with reference images for consistent characters.'
---

Black Forest Labs makes the FLUX image models. MagicBook uses FLUX for illustrations: pages, covers and character sheets. FLUX models can take reference images, so characters look the same from page to page.

## Get a key

Create a key in the [Black Forest Labs dashboard](https://dashboard.bfl.ai).

## Add it to MagicBook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers">
    Go to **AI → AI Providers** and find the **Black Forest Labs (FLUX)** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Press **Add key**, paste the key and press **Save and test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the models">
    Enable the FLUX models on [Image models](/admin/image-models). Choose one that shows **References** for the character sheet job.
  </Step>
</Steps>
