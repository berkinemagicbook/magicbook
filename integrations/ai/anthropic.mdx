---
title: 'Anthropic'
description: 'Connect Anthropic for long-form writing and editing with Claude models.'
---

Anthropic provides Claude models for writing. MagicBook uses them for planning, scenes, chapters and editing text. Anthropic does not offer image or voice models, so pictures and narration use other providers.

## Get a key

Create a key at [platform.claude.com/settings/keys](https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys).

## Add it to MagicBook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers">
    Go to **AI → AI Providers** and find the **Anthropic** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Press **Add key**, paste the key and press **Save and test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the models">
    Add and enable the Claude models on [Text models](/admin/text-models). Set the credits per 1,000 words for each one.
  </Step>
</Steps>
