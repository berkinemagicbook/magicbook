---
title: 'Recraft'
description: 'Connect Recraft for picture models that suit covers, vector art and line art.'
---

Recraft provides image models for covers, vector art and line art. MagicBook uses them for pictures, and line-art models suit the **Black-and-white line art (coloring pages)** art style.

## Get a key

Create a key in the [Recraft API portal](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/api-reference/api-portal).

## Add it to MagicBook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers">
    Go to **AI → AI Providers** and find the **Recraft** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Press **Add key**, paste the key and press **Save and test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the models">
    Enable the Recraft models on [Image models](/admin/image-models).
  </Step>
</Steps>
