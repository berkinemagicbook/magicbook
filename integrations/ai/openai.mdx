OpenAI covers three jobs in MagicBook:

- **Writing**: GPT models for planning, writing and editing text.
- **Pictures**: GPT Image models for pages, covers and character sheets.
- **Safety**: the moderation model that checks text and images for every audience. It is free to call.

## Get a key

Create a key at [platform.openai.com/api-keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys). Copy it when it is shown, because OpenAI does not show it again.

## Add it to MagicBook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers">
    Go to **AI → AI Providers** and find the **OpenAI** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Press **Add key**, paste the key and press **Save and test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the models">
    Enable the models you want on [Text models](/admin/text-models) and [Image models](/admin/image-models).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Save an OpenAI key even if you use other providers for writing. Without it, the safety check cannot run, and children's and teen books cannot be generated. See [Safety](/admin/safety).
