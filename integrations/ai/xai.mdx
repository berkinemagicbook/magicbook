---
title: 'xAI Grok'
description: 'Connect xAI for writing and planning with Grok models.'
---

xAI provides Grok models for writing. MagicBook uses them for planning and writing text. xAI does not offer image or voice models in MagicBook.

## Get a key

Create a key in the [xAI console](https://console.x.ai).

## Add it to MagicBook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers">
    Go to **AI → AI Providers** and find the **xAI Grok** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Press **Add key**, paste the key and press **Save and test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the models">
    Add and enable the Grok models on [Text models](/admin/text-models).
  </Step>
</Steps>
