---
title: 'Google Gemini'
description: 'Connect Google Gemini for writing and for the image models that keep characters consistent.'
---

Google Gemini covers two jobs in MagicBook:

- **Writing**: Gemini models for planning, scenes and editing.
- **Pictures**: Gemini image models, which keep characters consistent from reference sheets.

## Get a key

Create a key in [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey).

## Add it to MagicBook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers">
    Go to **AI → AI Providers** and find the **Google Gemini** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Press **Add key**, paste the key and press **Save and test**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the models">
    Enable Gemini text models on [Text models](/admin/text-models), and Gemini image models on [Image models](/admin/image-models). Gemini image models that accept references can draw character sheets.
  </Step>
</Steps>
