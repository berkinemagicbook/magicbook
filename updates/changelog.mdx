## 1.0

The first public release of MagicBook.

### Books and modes

- **Storyland** for children: picture books made by tapping pictures, with Inkling the owl reading each step aloud. A parent can lock the app into kid mode with a PIN.
- **Family** for parents: personal stories for a child, started from a recipe card. Picture books, short stories and chapter books.
- **Writer's Room** for authors: Spark, Shape, Plot, Cast & World, Story Room and the manuscript editor, with a Story Journal and version history.
- **Picture studio**: pages drawn in one art style, with characters kept consistent by character sheets, and a cover.
- **Reader**: read a book page by page, listen with narration, download a PDF and share a private link.

### AI and voices

- Writing with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and xAI. Pictures with Google Gemini, OpenAI, FLUX, Ideogram and Recraft.
- Narration and voice input with ElevenLabs, plus a Voices library for customers.
- Safety checks by audience, with a moderation log for admins.
- Credits: each AI step holds its estimated cost and charges the actual cost when it finishes.

### Library and account

- Bookshelf with covers or spines, search and sorting.
- Children profiles for Family books, and a media library for images.
- Account settings with two-factor authentication, notifications and support tickets.

### Admin

- AI providers, text and image models, voices, safety, generation activity and book limits.
- Users, system notifications, system status, registration and social login, SMTP, GDPR cookie settings, website content, blog, languages, plugins and themes.
- Two bundled frontend themes, Writer and Fairytale.

### Plugins

- **SaaS plugin** for plans, credits, payment gateways, invoices, subscriptions and affiliates.
- **Audiobook Studio** for full-cast audiobooks with M4B and ACX export.
- **Voice Cloning** for cloned voices from a recording booth or uploaded audio.
