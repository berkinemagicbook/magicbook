---
title: 'Support tickets'
description: 'Read and answer customer support tickets, and see how many are open, pending, resolved or closed.'
---

**Support → Tickets** lists every support ticket customers have opened. Customers open tickets under **Help → Support** in the app. See [Support](/account/support).

## The list

The top cards count the tickets by status: **Open Tickets**, **Pending Tickets**, **Resolved Tickets** and **Closed Tickets**. Select a card to filter the list by that status.

Select a ticket to read the conversation and reply. Set the ticket to **Resolved** when you have fixed the problem, or **Closed** when the conversation is over.

## Priority and category

Each ticket shows its **Category** (Technical, Billing, Account, General or Feature Request) and its **Priority** (Low, Medium, High or Urgent). Urgent tickets are also shown on the admin dashboard.

## Attachments

Customers can attach a JPG, JPEG or PNG screenshot up to 5 MB. Open an attachment from the ticket to view it.
