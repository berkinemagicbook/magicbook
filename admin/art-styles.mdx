---
title: 'Art styles'
description: 'Add, edit and preview the picture styles customers choose from for their picture books.'
---

**Books → Art styles** holds the picture styles customers choose from. A style sets the look of every picture drawn in it, such as watercolour or crayon.

## Add a style

Press **Add a style** and fill in:

- **Name**: the name customers see.
- **Short description**: one line under the name.
- **Style prompt**: the wording added to every picture in this style. Describe the medium, colours and line, for example "soft watercolour with loose edges".
- **Avoid**: things the pictures should not show, such as "photographic detail".
- **Offer for children's books**: turn on to show the style to children's books.
- **Black-and-white line art (coloring pages)**: turn on for line art that children can colour in.

Press **Save style** to add the style, or **Cancel** to close the form.

## Preview a style

Every style draws the same scene, so you can compare them side by side. Press **Draw preview** to make the preview with your image model. **Redraw preview** makes a new one. Previews use the credits of an image model, so draw them once per style.

## Edit and hide styles

Press **Edit** on a style to change its fields. A style appears to customers only when it is set up with a preview. Remove a style you no longer offer, or switch off **Offer for children's books** to hide it from Storyland.

Picture books use the style chosen on the page where the book is drawn. See [Picture studio](/user-guide/picture-studio).
