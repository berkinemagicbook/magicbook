title: 'Book settings'
description: 'Set the longest picture books, chapter books and novels the site allows, and whether parents may use a child photo in Family books.'
---

**Books → Book settings** holds the rules that apply to every book on the site, whatever the plan. Each plan can set a lower limit, but never a higher one.

## Book length maximums

Each format has one maximum, the **ceiling**:

| Format | Measured in | Default ceiling | Highest you can set |
|---|---|---|---|
| Picture book | Pages | 40 | 200 |
| Chapter book and short story | Words | 15,000 | 300,000 |
| Novel | Words | 100,000 | 300,000 |

Change a ceiling and press **Save maximums**. A customer's book can never be longer than the ceiling, even on the highest plan.

The effective limit for a customer is the lower of the ceiling and the plan's limit. For example, a plan that allows 60 pages still gets only 40 when the ceiling is 40. See [Plans](/saas/plans).

Shape in the Writer's Room shows the limit that applies to each book.

## Family photos

**Let parents use a photo of their child** allows a parent to upload one photo of a child for a closer likeness in a Family book. The photo is used once to draw the character sheet, then deleted. The customer's plan must also include **Child as the hero from a photo**. Leave this off if you do not want children's photos uploaded to your server.

## Default values

Ceilings start at the defaults above. The defaults are the values used after a fresh install.

<Note>
Changing a ceiling does not change books that are already longer. It only limits new additions to them.
</Note>
