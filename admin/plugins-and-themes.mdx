title: 'Plugins and themes'
description: 'Install, update and remove plugins and themes from the marketplace, and activate the themes that come with MagicBook.'
---

**Platform → Plugins** and **Platform → Themes** list the extensions you can add to MagicBook. Each card shows the extension, its price, and its state.

## Plugins

Use the tabs to narrow the list: **All**, **Installed**, **Paid**, **Free**, **Update Available** and **Free for Extended**. Search with **Search your plugins...**.

- **Install** adds a free plugin. Paid plugins show **Buy Now**, which opens the checkout.
- **Update** installs a newer version when **Update Available** shows.
- **Manage** opens the plugin's settings page.
- **Uninstall** removes the plugin's files. Your data stays in the database. Confirm when asked.

A plugin marked **Extended** needs an Extended License to install. The **Free for Extended** label marks plugins that are free for buyers of an Extended License. See [Extended license](/license/extended-license).

## Themes

The themes page lists the public and dashboard themes, with the tabs **All**, **Frontend**, **Dashboard**, **Free** and **Included**. **Live Preview** shows a theme before you use it.

MagicBook ships two frontend themes, **Writer** and **Fairytale**, marked **Included**. They do not need a purchase. Press **Activate Theme** to use one. The site's default theme is used again when you activate it.

Themes you buy follow the same steps: **Buy Now**, then **Install Theme**. Press **Update Theme** when a newer version shows.

## Bundles

Two bundles can be bought on the same page:

- **Premium VIP Support** is a monthly subscription for priority support in the ticket queue.
- **Premier Package Bundle** is a one-time purchase. It unlocks every paid plugin and theme, including the updates for them.

## Installing needs your license

Plugins and themes are downloaded with the license saved on **Platform → Activation**. Keep the license active. See [License activation](/license/activation).
