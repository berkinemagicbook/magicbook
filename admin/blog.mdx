The public blog is managed under **Frontend Settings → Blogs Manager**. Comments are reviewed under **Blog comments** on the same section.

## Posts

The **Blogs Manager** lists every post with its status: **Published**, **Draft** or **Archived**. Use **Search posts...** to find one, and the **All statuses** filter to narrow the list.

- **New Post** starts a post.
- **Edit post** opens a post to change it.
- **View on site** opens the published post on the public blog.
- **Mark as featured** highlights a post. Select the star again to remove it.
- **Delete post** removes the post and all its comments. This cannot be undone.

Each post shows its category, the reading time in minutes and its views.

## Comments

Readers can comment on published posts. The comments are listed under **Blog comments**, where you review them.

## Public blog

Published posts are shown on the public blog at `/blog`. Search engines index them with the SEO settings you set on [Website content](/admin/website-content).
