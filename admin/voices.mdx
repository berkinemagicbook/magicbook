**AI → Voices** controls narration and voice input. These sections appear only after you save a working ElevenLabs key on [AI providers](/admin/ai-providers).

## Narration

- **Model** and **Audio quality**: the ElevenLabs model and quality used for narration.
- **Credits per 1,000 characters**: what customers pay for narration. A 300-page novel is about 600,000 characters.
- **Default narrator**: used when a reader has not picked one. The first voice in the list is used when none is set.

## Voice input

- **Offer voice input**: turns on speech input in Storyland, where children can say their story out loud. When it is off, the option is hidden everywhere.
- **Credits per recording**: each recording is at most one minute long.

## Narrators

**Narrators** lists the voices from your ElevenLabs account. Press **Get voices from ElevenLabs** to load them.

- Switch a voice on to offer it to customers. Switched-off voices are hidden everywhere.
- Mark a voice **For children** to offer it in Storyland and for children's books.
- **Move up** and **Move down** set the order customers see.

Customers see the switched-on voices on the **Voices** page in their library. See [Voices](/user-guide/voices).

## Inkling's voice in Storyland

Inkling reads the steps in Storyland. Press **Record Inkling's lines** to record Inkling with one of your voices, so children hear the same voice every time. Until you record them, Inkling uses the browser's own voice. **Remove recordings** deletes all recorded lines, after you confirm.
