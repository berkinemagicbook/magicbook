**Backend Settings → GDPR Settings** controls the cookie consent banner on the public site.

## Cookie banner

- **Enable GDPR Cookies** shows the consent banner to visitors. Turn it on for visitors in regions that require consent.
- **Cookie Valid Days** sets how long a visitor's choice is remembered.
- **Enable Dark Mode** shows the banner in dark colours.
- **Cookie Consent Modal Layout** and **Cookie Consent Modal Position** set how the banner looks and where it sits.
- **Preferences Modal Layout** and **Preferences Modal Position** set the same for the window where visitors choose which cookies to allow.

The page also has **Disable Page Interaction** and **Hide from Bots** switches. Read the description printed under each one on the page before you turn it on, because the descriptions state exactly what they change.

Press **Save** to keep the changes.

## Privacy policy and terms

The text of the privacy policy and the terms is edited on [Website content → Pages](/admin/website-content). Customers can reach them from the public site.
