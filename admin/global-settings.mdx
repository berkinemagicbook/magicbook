**Backend Settings → Global Settings** holds the site-wide details and the Google services MagicBook can use.

## Website

- **Website Name** and **Website URL**: the name and address of your site.
- **Default Main Theme**: the theme visitors see first, **Light Theme** or **Dark Theme**.
- **Time Zone**: the time zone the site uses.

## Google reCAPTCHA v3

Turn on **Enable Google reCaptcha v3** to protect the sign-up and contact forms from bots. Paste the **Google reCaptcha Site Key** and the **Google reCaptcha Secret Key**. Create the keys in your Google reCAPTCHA admin console.

See [Google reCAPTCHA](/advanced/google-recaptcha).

## Google Analytics

- **Google Analytics for Homepage**: turn on **Enable Google Analytics for Homepage** and paste the **Google Analytics Tracking ID**. This tracks visitors to the public site.
- **Google Analytics for Admin Dashboard**: turn on **Enable Google Analytics for Admin Dashboard**, then paste the **Google Analytics Property ID** and upload the **Google Service Account Credentials** file. The admin dashboard then shows website traffic.

See [Google Analytics](/advanced/google-analytics).

## Google Maps

Turn on **Enable Google Maps** and paste the **Google Maps API Key**. The accounts dashboard uses it to draw the countries map. See [Google Maps](/advanced/google-maps).

Press **Save** to keep the changes.
