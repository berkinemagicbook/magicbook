title: 'Extended license'
description: 'Which Envato license your MagicBook purchase carries, how to see it in the admin panel, and when to check the terms.'
---

MagicBook is sold through Envato under one of Envato's standard licenses. The license type decides how you may use the software, for example whether you can charge customers for access to it.

## See your license type

After you activate the license, open **Platform → Activation**. The **License Type** field shows the type the license server returned for your purchase. Check it against your Envato receipt.

## Check the terms

Envato's license terms change over time and apply to each sale. Before you run MagicBook for paying customers, or add MagicBook to a service you sell, read the license terms on the Envato item page and in Envato's help center. If you are not sure which license covers your use, ask Envato support before you launch.

## Plugins

Some plugins can be marked as free for buyers with an Extended License. Those plugins show the **Free for Extended** label in **Platform → Plugins**. See [Plugins and themes](/admin/plugins-and-themes).
